**Organized play is on the way.** We're building a year-round calendar of open
tables and a competitive season you can climb by playing the game well.

## What's coming

**Season 1 — Foundation.** A 13-week season built around one published scenario
each week, with open tables scheduled so the Americas, Europe/Africa, and
Asia-Pacific each get a prime-time block. The season builds from open tables to a
scenario season with a handful of cups, and finishes with a championship.

Every table is cooperative — you and the field take on the mastermind — and a
solo player can always fill an empty seat with a bot ally.

## The promise

- **Free to play.** Open play and every scored event carry no entry fee.
- **Standing is earned, not bought.** Season standing comes from playing the
  weekly scenario well — never from buy-in, hours logged, or anything you can
  purchase. No pay-to-enter, no VIP tables, no advantage for sale.
- **Play from anywhere.** Windows are published in your local time, with a bot
  ally ready whenever you'd rather not wait for a full table.
