Legendary Arena is a skill-first, web-based deck-building system —
heroes assembled for the moment, scenarios that punish the wrong call,
standings earned across sessions rather than ground out across hours.
This is the project's first public word.

## What this site is

The marketing site you're reading is the front door. The card registry
runs at
[cards.barefootbetters.com](https://cards.barefootbetters.com/). The
game client opens at
[play.legendary-arena.com](https://play.legendary-arena.com/) when
development is ready for it. The three properties run independently so
any one can fail without taking the others down.

## Why a blog

Most of what's interesting about building Legendary Arena happens below
the surface: a deterministic engine that produces the same state from
the same inputs, a brand contract that runs as cross-origin CSS so
visual identity stays consistent across properties, three independent
sites coordinated through nothing but HTTP. The blog is where that work
gets framed — not as release notes, but as the design choices behind
them.

## What to expect

Posts when there's something worth saying, not on a schedule. The bar
is "would a stranger who plays deck-builders learn something." That's
the only filter.

The arena is being built. This site is where that work gets written
down.
