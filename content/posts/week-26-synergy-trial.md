The Foundations Trial tested whether your basics were sound. The Synergy Trial tests whether you can build something powerful under pressure. This challenge adds constraints that force you to apply every concept from Q2: synergy identification, engine construction, scaling, consistency, and the discipline to avoid dilution.

## The challenge rules

Play three games with the following constraints:

### Constraint 1: Limited recruitment budget

You may recruit a maximum of twelve cards across the entire game (not counting starter cards). Every recruit must be intentional. There is no room for speculative purchases, dilution, or "maybe this will be useful" recruits.

This constraint tests your [keep/buy/skip](/posts/week-06-hand-evaluation/) discipline and your ability to avoid [dilution](/posts/week-24-dilution/).

### Constraint 2: Declare your synergy type

Before your first recruit, declare which [synergy type](/posts/week-14-synergy-types/) your deck will be built around. Every recruit should serve that declaration. At the end of the game, evaluate whether your deck actually achieved the synergy you declared or whether it drifted.

This constraint tests your synergy awareness and focus.

### Constraint 3: Identify your engine at mid-game

At the halfway point of the game (you define where that is), pause and write down your engine components — fuel (draw), conversion (synergy triggers), and output (damage or recruit). If you cannot identify all three, your engine is incomplete.

This constraint tests your [engine building](/posts/week-15-engine-building/) intentionality.

## Scoring

After each game, score yourself on five criteria using the 1–3 scale:

1. **Synergy execution.** Did your deck achieve the synergy type you declared? (1–3)
2. **Engine activation.** Did your engine produce compound turns — turns where draw into synergy into output created more value than the sum of individual cards? (1–3)
3. **Recruitment discipline.** Did you stay within twelve recruits? Were all twelve intentional? (1–3)
4. **Scaling.** Did your damage output grow over the course of the game, or was it flat? (1–3)
5. **Consistency.** Were your turns reliably productive, or did you experience dead turns with no useful cards? (1–3)

Maximum score per game: 15. Maximum total across three games: 45.

## Interpreting your score

**36–45: Synergy mastery.** Your Q2 skills are strong. You build with intention, scale effectively, and maintain discipline under constraints. You are ready for Q3: Competitive Season.

**25–35: Functional with room to grow.** You understand the concepts but application under constraints exposed gaps. Review the lowest-scoring criteria and replay the relevant Q2 posts.

**Below 25: Revisit Q2 fundamentals.** The constraints revealed that the concepts are not yet reliable under pressure. Focus on synergy identification and engine building before moving to competitive play.

## The transition to Q3

The Power & Synergy series is complete. You can now build focused, synergistic decks with engines that compound, scale intentionally, and avoid the dilution that kills most players' strategies.

Q3 — Competitive Season — shifts the focus from construction to competition. Building a good deck is necessary. Playing that deck against opponents who also build good decks is a different challenge entirely. Next week begins with [matchup thinking](/posts/week-27-matchups/) — the shift from "my deck does X" to "this game needs Y."

The arena is about to get serious.
