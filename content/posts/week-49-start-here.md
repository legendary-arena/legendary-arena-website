Welcome to Legendary Arena. Whether you received a gift, joined a friend's game, or downloaded the app on a whim — this is your fast track. Three weeks. Three skills. By the end, you will be building focused decks, playing with intention, and ready for your first competitive challenge.

## Week 1: Build your first deck

Your first week focuses on the most important skill: building a deck that does something on purpose.

**Read:** [The 7-Point Deck Health Check](/posts/week-01-deck-checklist/) — a step-by-step process for assembling a focused deck.

**Key concepts:**
- Write a strategy sentence before you start. One sentence that describes what your deck does.
- Map your resource curve — low, mid, high cost cards distributed for consistent draws.
- Every card fills a role: recruiter, attacker, or utility. Balance them.

**Practice:** Build one deck using the checklist. Play one full game with it. Do not change the deck mid-game — learn what works and what does not.

**Milestone:** You can build a deck from the checklist without referencing it.

## Week 2: Play with intention

Your second week focuses on what you do during the game — turn execution and decision-making.

**Read:** [The 5-Step Turn](/posts/week-05-turn-sequencing/) and [Keep, Buy, Skip](/posts/week-06-hand-evaluation/).

**Key concepts:**
- Play draw effects before committing attacks or recruits. Information before commitment.
- For every card in the recruitment row, classify it: keep (serves your strategy), buy (fills a gap), or skip (not needed). Most cards should be skips.
- Unspent resources are wasted resources. But buying a bad card is worse than wasting resources.

**Practice:** Play two games this week. In both, physically sort your hand before playing: draw effects left, attack right. Say "keep," "buy," or "skip" for every recruitment decision.

**Milestone:** The five-step turn feels natural. You skip cards without guilt.

## Week 3: Take your first challenge

Your third week tests what you have learned with a simplified version of the [Foundations Trial](/posts/week-13-foundations-trial/).

**The beginner benchmark:** Play three games. Score each on a 1–3 scale:

1. **Deck focus.** Was your deck built around a clear strategy sentence? (1 = scattered, 3 = focused)
2. **Turn execution.** Did you follow the five-step turn consistently? (1 = forgot often, 3 = automatic)
3. **Recruitment discipline.** Did you skip cards that did not serve your strategy? (1 = bought everything, 3 = bought only what you needed)

**Maximum score:** 27 (three criteria across three games).

**What your score means:**
- 20–27: Strong start. You are ready for the full Fundamentals series (Weeks 1–13).
- 13–19: Solid foundation with room to grow. Replay Weeks 1 and 2, then continue.
- Below 13: Normal for your first three games. Replay the three-week path once more before moving forward.

## Where to go next

After the three-week path, you have options:

**Continue the Fundamentals series (Weeks 1–13).** This fills in the skills the three-week path introduced: threat assessment, early-game priorities, mid-game pivots, endgame closing, common traps, and co-op roles.

**Jump to the topic that interests you most.** If competitive play excites you, skip to [Your First Tournament](/posts/week-04-first-tournament/). If deck synergy fascinates you, start [Q2: Power & Synergy](/posts/week-14-synergy-types/).

**Just play.** The three-week path gives you enough foundation to enjoy the game and improve through experience. The rest of the series is here when you want it.
