The holiday season is about community, competition, and proving what you have built over the year. The Holiday Ladder is a six-week series of escalating challenges that test your skills, reward consistency, and give you something to aim for heading into the year-end championship.

## How the ladder works

Six weekly challenges, each harder than the last. You earn points for completing each challenge, with bonus points for performance quality. Points accumulate across all six weeks. The leaderboard tracks cumulative scores.

### Week 1 — The Foundation Check

Replay the [Foundations Trial](/posts/week-13-foundations-trial/) five-scenario benchmark. Your score (out of 45) is your entry point. This establishes your baseline and reminds you where your fundamentals stand.

**Points:** Your benchmark score (up to 45).

### Week 2 — The Lean Build

Complete a game with a maximum deck size of twenty cards (excluding starters). Win the game within this constraint.

**Points:** 10 for completion. Bonus 5 for winning with fifteen or fewer cards (after thinning).

### Week 3 — The Speed Run

Complete a game in under thirty minutes (real time). All standard rules apply.

**Points:** 10 for completion under thirty minutes. Bonus 5 for under twenty minutes.

### Week 4 — The Handicap

Complete a game without recruiting any card costing more than four. No finishers, no expensive power cards — win with mid-range efficiency.

**Points:** 10 for completion. Bonus 5 for winning without any card above cost three.

### Week 5 — The Teamwork Trial

Complete a co-op game where each player is assigned a specific [role](/posts/week-12-coop-roles/) and must stay in that role for the entire game. No role-swapping.

**Points:** 10 for team completion. Bonus 5 for perfect role adherence (no off-role actions).

### Week 6 — The Gauntlet

Complete three games back-to-back with no break between them. Each game uses a different [archetype](/posts/week-35-archetypes/). Score is based on total performance across all three.

**Points:** 10 per game won (up to 30). Bonus 10 for winning all three.

## Leaderboard and prizes

The leaderboard is cumulative. Maximum possible score: 135.

Participation at any level is the point — completing even one challenge puts you on the board. The ladder is designed so that players of all skill levels can participate and progress. The early challenges are accessible; the late ones are where competitive players separate themselves.

Recognition categories:
- **Iron Arena:** Complete all six challenges (any score)
- **Silver Arena:** Score 80 or above
- **Gold Arena:** Score 110 or above

## Try this

Start with Week 1 — the Foundation Check. Replay the benchmark and submit your score. You have six weeks. Pace yourself, and let each challenge build on the skills you have developed all year.
