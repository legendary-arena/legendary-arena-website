Fifty-two weeks. Four series. One journey from "how do I build a deck" to "how do I win a championship." This final post of the year looks back at what the community accomplished, looks forward at what is coming next, and gives you a clear path into the next twelve months.

## The year in review

### What you learned

**Q1: Fundamentals.** Deck construction, resource curves, Scenario scouting, turn sequencing, hand evaluation, threat assessment, early-game priorities, mid-game pivots, endgame closing, common traps, co-op roles, and the Foundations Trial. The base layer that every skill builds on.

**Q2: Power & Synergy.** Synergy types, engine building, card draw, tempo, scaling damage, stabilization, soft and hard combos, contested drafting, consistency, dilution, tournament prep, and the Synergy Trial. The mechanical mastery that separates intentional builders from card collectors.

**Q3: Competitive Season.** Matchup thinking, flex slots, playing from behind and ahead, reading opponents, risk management, decision speed, practice routines, archetypes, post-game review, mental game, tournament rules, and championship lessons. The competitive toolkit that turns a good player into a tournament contender.

**Q4: Mastery & Events.** Advanced sequencing, economy transitions, hybrid decks, Plan B design, teaching, community events, deck clinics, meta analysis, new player onboarding, and finals execution. The expert layer and community-building that sustain the game long-term.
