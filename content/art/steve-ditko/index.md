Steve Ditko co-created two of Marvel's most enduring heroes with Stan Lee.
In *Amazing Fantasy #15* (1962) he designed Spider-Man — the full-mask
costume with its large white eye lenses, the web-shooters, the spider-signal,
and the agile, neurotic body language that made Peter Parker a flawed,
everyday hero. He drew the first 38 issues of *The Amazing Spider-Man* and
shaped its early supporting cast and rogues' gallery, from J. Jonah Jameson to
Doctor Octopus and the Green Goblin.

A year later, in *Strange Tales #110* (1963), he co-created Doctor Strange and
gave the Sorcerer Supreme his visual and conceptual identity: the mystic
gestures, otherworldly dimensions, and abstract, psychedelic landscapes that
made the feature a favorite of 1960s counterculture readers. Ditko's later
Marvel work includes redesigning Iron Man's classic red-and-gold armor and
co-creating Speedball and Squirrel Girl.
