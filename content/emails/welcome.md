## Your first win (right now, 60 seconds)

Here is a tip you can use in your very next game:

**Before your first recruit, write one sentence that describes what your deck does.** Example: "Build economy fast, then recruit one finisher to close by turn 10." That sentence becomes your filter. Every card you consider either serves that sentence or stays out.

That is it. One sentence. Sixty seconds. It eliminates the most common deckbuilding mistake: buying good cards that do not belong together.
