## Task description

The diorama line is positioned as collectible / educational merchandise. WP-024 is putting a product image + CTA on the diorama landing page. This brief surfaces:

1. **Merchandising copy** — How to frame the diorama product line (collectible vs. STEM/educational vs. gift angle)
2. **Lead magnets** — Free content that captures diorama-interested visitors (PDFs, guides, setup tips)
3. **Email funnel** — A sequence to move "interested" → "bought"
4. **Shop integration** — How diorama fits into the existing shop structure

### Section 1: Product framing (3 variants)

Generate 3 variants that position the diorama differently:

**Variant A: Collectible angle**
> "Build and display a working physical diorama with magnetic LED figures from the Legendary Arena universe. Handcrafted, detailed, and designed for serious collectors and fans."

**Variant B: STEM/educational angle**
> "A hands-on magnetic diorama kit that teaches circuit design, engineering principles, and electronics assembly — all while you're building an iconic Legendary Arena scene."

**Variant C: Gift angle**
> "The perfect gift for Legendary Arena fans, card-game enthusiasts, or anyone who loves hands-on building projects. No soldering required; play with it out of the box or customize it yourself."

For each, note:
- **Target audience:** Who buys this?
- **Price sensitivity:** What price point does this frame suggest?
- **Sales message:** What objection does this address?

### Section 2: Lead magnets (5 ideas)

Generate 5 lead-magnet ideas that capture diorama-interested visitors before they buy:

1. **"Diorama Building Guide PDF"** (5–10 pages)
   - What it covers: Tools needed, step-by-step assembly, customization ideas
   - Offer: Free download (email gate — "Enter your email to download")
   - Why: Removes friction for first-time builders
   - CTR boost: 15–25% (estimated)

2. **"LED Circuit Customization Workbook"** (3–5 pages)
   - What it covers: How to adjust LED color/brightness, wire custom circuits, troubleshoot
   - Offer: Free download
   - Why: Speaks to the STEM angle; appeals to DIY builders
   - CTR boost: 10–15%

3. **"Setup & Display Tips"** (2–3 pages)
   - What it covers: Optimal lighting, display shelf arrangements, weatherproofing
   - Offer: Free download
   - Why: Collectors want to display it beautifully
   - CTR boost: 5–10%

4. **"Diorama Customization Lookbook"** (image-heavy, 4–6 pages)
   - What it covers: Community builder photos, painting ideas, terrain modifications
   - Offer: Free download + community showcase submission
   - Why: Social proof + engagement
   - CTR boost: 20–30%

5. **"Legendary Arena Lore + Diorama Connection"** (PDF or blog series)
   - What it covers: Lore behind the scene(s) in the diorama kit
   - Offer: Free download or blog series (email signup for the series)
   - Why: Deepens the Legendary universe connection
   - CTR boost: 10–15%

For each lead magnet:
- Note the effort (30 min PDF vs. full photo lookbook)
- Note the likely conversion (PDF downloads = 20–40%; email gates = 15–25%)
- Note the best placement (blog sidebar, diorama landing page, email footer)

### Section 3: Email funnel (4-email sequence)

Generate a 4-email sequence for visitors who download a lead magnet or express interest:

**Email 1: Welcome + deliver the lead magnet**
- **Trigger:** Immediate (on download or signup)
- **Goal:** Confirm opt-in, deliver promise, set tone
- **CTA:** "Start building" link to diorama page
- **Subject line variant:** "Here's your Diorama Building Guide — plus an exclusive offer inside"

**Email 2: Social proof + urgency (day 3)**
- **Goal:** Show that others are buying and building; create FOMO
- **Content:** Community photos, testimonials, "limited quantities" if applicable
- **CTA:** "Shop the diorama kit"
- **Subject line variant:** "See what builders are creating (and how you can too)"

**Email 3: Objection handling (day 7)**
- **Goal:** Address common hesitations (price, time to build, technical skill)
- **Content:** "Is it hard to build?" / "How much does it cost?" / "Will it last?"
- **CTA:** "Read the FAQ" or "Shop now"
- **Subject line variant:** "Questions about the diorama? We've answered them."

**Email 4: Final push + scarcity (day 14)**
- **Goal:** Convert remaining holdouts
- **Content:** "Last chance" or "Popular items running low"
- **CTA:** "Get yours before they're gone"
- **Subject line variant:** "Your diorama kit is waiting (limited stock)"

For each email:
- Note tone (playful, technical, social, urgent — mix them)
- Note the CTA placement (top, middle, bottom, or all three)

### Section 4: Shop integration

Generate guidance on how diorama fits into the larger shop structure:

**Storefront layout:**
- **Main shop page:** Features diorama as a highlighted product (top of fold? Hero section?)
- **Category:** Create a "Collectibles" or "Dioramas" category if shop supports it
- **Related products:** What else might a diorama buyer want? (Display stands, paint kits, protective cases)
- **Upsells:** What's the natural cross-sell? (Digital assets? Lore content? Exclusive diorama variants)

**Product page structure (for each diorama variant):**
- **Hero image:** High-res, lit, showing the finished product
- **Product title:** Clear, benefit-forward (not just "Diorama Kit" but "Legendary Arena Magnetic Diorama — Light & Build Kit")
- **Short description:** 1–2 sentences, positioning variant (collectible / STEM / gift)
- **Feature bullets:** (e.g., "30+ magnetic pieces", "Customizable LED lighting", "No soldering required")
- **Price + CTA:** Clear, with shipping + variants clearly noted
- **Testimonials / reviews:** Once customers start buying and leaving reviews
- **Related products:** Accessories, protective cases, display stands

**Email integration:**
- Diorama shop page should have an email-signup CTA below the fold ("Sign up for restock notifications" or "Get tips & tricks in your inbox")
- Shop CTA should link back to the "Get started" guide or diorama landing page for context

## Do / Don't

**DO:**
- Think about the buyer's journey: awareness (lead magnet) → interest (social proof email) → consideration (objection handling) → decision (scarcity)
- Identify quick wins (e.g., "PDF guide" is fast to create; lookbook takes longer)
- Cross-reference to the diorama landing page (this brief is an extension, not a separate surface)
- Suggest email timing that doesn't feel spammy (3–14 days between emails is typical)
- Note which lead magnets are high-effort / high-return vs. low-effort / nice-to-have

**DON'T:**
- Speculate on inventory or pricing (that's the user's call)
- Design the shop page or emails (you're recommending strategy, not implementation)
- Assume the diorama is tied to the game launch (it's a product line, separate lifecycle)
- Suggest lead magnets that are too niche (they should appeal broadly to the target segment)
- Ignore the email + shop connection (funnel thinking, not siloed thinking)
