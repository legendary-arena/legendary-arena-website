# Community Advertising & Sponsorship Plan — Legendary player sites

**Scope:** how Legendary Arena reaches the engaged online Legendary player base
through the community sites and leagues — specifically
`legendarycardgame.com/community-events` and `legendaryleagues.com`. Executes the
**companion-platform / credibility wedge** in
[`04-ip-licensing-acquisition-plan.md`](04-ip-licensing-acquisition-plan.md) §5.
**Status:** Draft (pre-outreach). No spend or contact has been made.
**Last updated:** 2026-08-11

> **Gated, not go.** This plan **must not execute** ahead of two decisions in the
> licensing plan: **Phase 0 (retain counsel)** and the **public Marvel-build
> decision** (`04` §5 Immediate action / Risk 7). Advertising an *unlicensed
> Marvel* product into the rights-holders' own community is the single most
> exposure-*increasing* move available — see §3. This doc is the "how," ready for
> when those gates clear.

---

## 1. What these sites actually are (reviewed 2026-08-11)

**Neither site sells ad inventory, and neither is official Upper Deck.** "Buying
advertising" is the wrong frame — the real lever is **community sponsorship /
partnership**. Both orbit **Bageltop Games**, the de-facto hub of the online
Legendary community (content creator + Discord + tools).

- **`legendarycardgame.com/community-events`** — a **community-run fan hub**
  (Squarespace) covering Marvel / DC / Bond / Encounters Legendary. It *aggregates*
  community resources: Bageltop Games content + Discord, the Master-Strike card
  database, custom-card spreadsheets, ranking tools, and social links (Discord /
  YouTube / Facebook / BoardGameGeek). `[CONFIRM]` No contact form, ad rate card,
  or sponsorship page was visible on the page — outreach would go through Bageltop
  Games / the linked Discord.
- **`legendaryleagues.com`** — an **active organized-play platform** for Legendary
  (Marvel **and** DC content). Live features: Campaigns, Randomizer, Hero Browser,
  Stats, and running **Leagues** (Unlimited, Core Values, Adventure, Challenge
  Matches) with real 2026 registration/end dates and user accounts. It runs an
  **Extra Life charity fundraiser** (observed ~$2.3k of a $5.1k goal; ~$40k
  lifetime) and hosts a "Bageltop Games Randomizer League." `[CONFIRM]` who
  operates it and whether any sponsorship path is published; the About menu is
  documentation, not a contact page.

**Strategic read:** `legendaryleagues.com` is not just an ad target — it is a
**partial competitor and a partner candidate.** It already does randomizer, hero
browser, stats, campaigns, and leagues — the exact **companion-platform wedge**
`04` §5 describes. That reframes the opportunity (§4).

## 2. Why this audience is the right one

This is a bullseye for two goals at once:
- **User acquisition** — the most engaged, digital-friendly Legendary players
  alive, already playing leagues and using randomizers/trackers. Exactly Legendary
  Arena's target.
- **Credibility with Upper Deck** — this *is* the community UD cares about (more
  players, more engagement, organized play). Becoming a valued, visible, generous
  presence here is a direct deposit into the "earn a partnership, don't request a
  license" thesis (`04` §5).

---

## 3. The risk that gates everything: don't advertise an unlicensed Marvel product

Promoting the **current Marvel-branded build** to the core Legendary community —
which is Marvel-IP-adjacent and includes people connected to Upper Deck — is
`04` Risk 7 with the volume turned up: you would be *actively surfacing* the
unlicensed product to the exact people who could act on it. Guardrails:

- **Advertise only the license-clean / companion offering** (original / fantasy /
  verified-PD content, or the non-gameplay companion tooling), **never** the live
  Marvel gameplay build. See `04` §5 license-clean options.
- **Sequence after** the public-build decision and Phase-0 counsel. Counsel should
  see the creative and the landing page before any spend.
- **Trademark hygiene:** any placement uses Legendary Arena's own brand; do not
  imply endorsement by Upper Deck, Marvel, Bageltop, or Legendary Leagues, and do
  not use "Legendary®" / Marvel marks in ad creative without clearance.

---

## 4. The plan — sponsorship & partnership, tiered by risk and cost

Ordered low-risk/low-cost → higher-commitment. Each is contingent on §3.

1. **Goodwill entry — sponsor the Extra Life charity drive.** `legendaryleagues.com`
   runs an Extra Life fundraiser. A sponsorship/matching-donation is the cleanest,
   most welcome first contact: it buys goodwill and visibility in the community
   with **zero** product-promotion risk, and it signals "we're here to support the
   ecosystem," not extract from it. Best possible on-ramp to a relationship with
   the operators / Bageltop Games.
2. **Community presence — the Bageltop Games Discord + content collab.** Since both
   sites route through Bageltop, the highest-leverage "advertising" is a
   relationship with Bageltop Games: a sponsored video / stream, a Discord
   partnership or sponsored channel, or co-produced content once a license-clean
   build exists to show. Content collaboration with the community's central creator
   beats a banner nobody sells.
3. **League sponsorship / prize support.** Sponsor a season or provide prizes for a
   `legendaryleagues.com` league — brand exposure to registered, active players,
   framed as ecosystem support.
4. **Listing / cross-link on the fan hub.** Ask `legendarycardgame.com` (via
   Bageltop) to list Legendary Arena's **license-clean** offering among its
   community tools — earned placement through usefulness, not a media buy.
5. **Partner, don't just advertise (the real prize).** Because
   `legendaryleagues.com` overlaps our companion-platform wedge, the higher-value
   move may be **collaboration or consolidation** rather than competition —
   co-develop, integrate, or (an `04` §6 Endgame-D-style option) explore bringing
   the Bageltop / Legendary Leagues ecosystem and Legendary Arena together. That
   turns a would-be competitor into the community backbone of the credibility
   story. `[CONFIRM]` entirely — a relationship to explore, not a proposal to make
   cold.

## 5. Budget, targeting, measurement

- **Spend posture:** start **small and goodwill-first** (a charity sponsorship /
  a sponsored piece of content), not a large media commitment — there is no media
  inventory to buy, and the value is relationship + credibility, treated as a
  marketing investment (mirrors the loss-leader logic in the Monetization Model).
- **Landing target:** a **license-clean** Legendary Arena surface (or the companion
  tooling), never the Marvel build.
- **Measurement:** attribute with the canonical metrics in
  [`02-kpi-dictionary.md`](02-kpi-dictionary.md) (referrals, sign-ups, activated
  players, retention) — one definition set, so acquisition results feed the same
  traction story the UD pitch (`04` Phase 2) uses. Use UTM tags / a dedicated
  landing path per placement.

---

## 6. Sequence

1. `04` Phase 0 (counsel) + public-build decision clear the gate (§3).
2. Reach Bageltop Games / Legendary Leagues via Discord — **lead with the Extra
   Life sponsorship** (goodwill), not a pitch.
3. Layer in content collaboration / league sponsorship pointing at the
   license-clean surface.
4. Explore the partner/consolidation conversation (§4.5) only once a relationship
   exists.
5. Feed acquisition + engagement results (per `02`) into the `04` credibility
   milestones and the UD partnership deck.
