## Subject line

Read your resource curve in 3 layers

## Hook

Your resource curve is the heartbeat of your deck. Read it wrong, and even the strongest cards in the arena cannot save a stalled hand. This week: how to diagnose and shape the curve that powers every turn.

## Tip / Strategy

The resource curve is the distribution of card costs across your deck. Line up every card by cost, and the shape that emerges reveals how your deck performs from opening hand through the final fight against the Mastermind.

A balanced curve peaks in the mid-range (3–5 cost) and tapers at both ends. This shape produces consistent, playable hands at every stage. When your curve is top-heavy — loaded with expensive finishers — your early turns stall and the Scheme escalates before you gain traction. When it runs flat, you draw mediocre hands that never reach the power threshold you need.

Shaping the curve is an active discipline: thin aggressively early to raise average draw quality, recruit with purpose in the mid-game by filling gaps instead of stacking peaks, and limit high-cost finishers to two or three at most. The Scenario you face should also influence your curve shape — fast Schemes demand a left-shifted curve, while durable Masterminds require more finishing power.

## Challenge

Before your next session, lay out your deck by cost and count the cards at each point. Identify whether your curve peaks in the mid-range or skews heavy. Remove one card from your most crowded cost band and add one that fills a gap. Track the difference across three sessions.
