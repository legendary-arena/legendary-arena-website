## Subject line

Your first deck starts here (7 steps)

## Hook

A strong deck is not a pile of powerful cards — it is a focused weapon built with intention. This week's strategy breakdown walks you through the seven decisions that separate winning decks from scattered ones.

## Tip / Strategy

Building your first Legendary Arena deck demands a framework, not guesswork. The most important step comes before you touch a single card: define your strategy in one sentence. That sentence becomes the filter for every decision that follows.

Once your strategy is clear, the rest falls into place. Map your resource curve across three bands — low-cost foundation, mid-cost engine, high-cost finishers — so you draw playable hands consistently, not occasionally. Assign every card a role (recruiter, attacker, or utility) and balance those roles against your strategy.

The mistake most new players make is building their deck in isolation. The Scenario you face determines which cards earn their slot. Scout the threat before you lock in your build, cut any card that does not serve your strategy sentence, and treat your first version as a hypothesis — not a finished product.

## Challenge

Build a new deck using the seven-step checklist. After one session, identify the two weakest cards in your deck — the ones that sat dead in your hand — and replace them. Measure how the revision changes your next session.
