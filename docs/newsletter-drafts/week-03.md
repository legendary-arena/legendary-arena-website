## Subject line

Scout the Scenario before you build

## Hook

The difference between a good player and a great one is not card selection. It is information. This week: how to read a Scenario in layers and let the threat shape your entire build.

## Tip / Strategy

Every Scenario in Legendary Arena defines a unique threat profile — a Mastermind with specific abilities, a Villain group with mechanics that punish or reward certain approaches, and a Scheme that sets the pace of the entire session. Building a deck without reading this profile first is building on assumptions.

Scouting means reading the Scenario in three layers before you draft a single card. First, study the Mastermind: its attack threshold tells you whether to prioritize heavy single hits or distributed damage, and its ability reveals which strategies it punishes. Second, examine the Villain group's escape effects and fight rewards to determine whether early aggression or controlled recruitment serves you better. Third, identify the Scheme's win/loss condition and twist frequency to gauge how much of your deck must address defense versus pure offense.

Armed with this intelligence, your deck checklist and resource curve shift from generic frameworks to precision tools. Your strategy sentence sharpens, your curve adjusts to the Scenario's pace, and your card roles rebalance against the specific threats you will face.

## Challenge

Pick a Scenario you have not played before. Spend two minutes reading the Mastermind, Villain group, and Scheme before building your deck. Write a one-sentence strategy that directly addresses the threat profile. After the session, evaluate whether your scouting changed the outcome compared to building blind.
