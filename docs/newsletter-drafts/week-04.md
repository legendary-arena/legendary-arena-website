## Subject line

Your first tournament starts with preparation

## Hook

Tournaments are where mastery is earned — not in practice, not in casual sessions, but under pressure against players who have done the same preparation. This week: everything you need before you register.

## Tip / Strategy

A Legendary Arena tournament demands consistency across multiple rounds. One strong session is not enough — you must perform against varied Scenarios, adapting your build to each new threat as it appears. The preparation that matters most happens before you enter.

Start by confirming your fundamentals are automatic. Building a focused deck from the checklist, reading a resource curve at a glance, and scouting a Scenario in under two minutes should all flow without hesitation. If any of those still require deliberation, sharpen them in casual sessions first. Then build a versatile core deck — a mid-range engine that handles a range of threats — and plan targeted adjustments for specific Scenario types as rounds are revealed.

The mental shift matters as much as the tactical one. Tournament pressure changes the weight of every decision. Losses become data points, not defeats — each one revealing a gap in your curve, your scouting, or your sequencing. The players at the top of the standings earned their place through repetition and the willingness to compete before they felt fully ready.

## Challenge

Register for your first tournament. Before round one, apply the full Fundamentals sequence: scout the Scenario, write your strategy sentence, build your deck from the checklist, and verify your resource curve. After the tournament, review your results and identify the single biggest improvement for your next entry.
